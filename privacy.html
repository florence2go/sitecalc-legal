Material Calc Privacy Policy

Version: 20 September 2026. Matches the App Store listing and the shipped iPhone app.

What we do not collect

Material Calc is a paid App Store download. There is no account, no login, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The app does not upload your projects or photos to a developer-operated server. We do not sell or share that data.

How data is stored

Projects, shopping lists, unit prices you type, and optional site photos stay on this iPhone (local SwiftData and the app’s local files). The app does not implement iCloud sync or a developer backend. If you delete a project in the app, its local photos are removed with it. Uninstalling the app clears the on-device store.

Camera and Photo Library

Camera and Photo Library access is only so you can attach a photo to a local project. Photos are not uploaded, analysed, or used to measure rooms. There is no AR measure and no microphone or speech recognition.

Purchases

You pay once on the App Store before download. After install, all ten calculators are included. There is no in-app purchase and no subscription. Apple handles payment. Material Calc does not receive your payment card details.

Children

Material Calc does not knowingly collect personal data from children and does not offer public chat, stranger interaction, or targeted ads.

Contact

For privacy help, use App Support on the App Store product page. There is no public community and no developer-hosted account.
