Material Calc App Support

Version: 21 September 2026. Matches the App Store listing and the shipped iPhone app.

How to reach us

Use App Support on the App Store product page. Feature ideas and bugs can also go in an App Store review. Until a public email is published, do not invent a contact address in the repo or review notes.

What the app does

Material Calc is an offline renovation material calculator. Pick a material, enter dimensions, see pack counts, save into a project, build a shopping list, optionally type unit prices, and export a quote PDF. Ten calculators are included after purchase. There is no account.

Not included: voice dictation, AR measuring, Bluetooth laser meters, iCloud sync, Home Screen widgets, or a built-in material price library.

Purchases

Paid App Store download, one-time purchase. No trial, no subscription, no in-app purchases. Apple handles payment. After you buy and install, every calculator is available. Re-download with the same Apple ID after changing phones.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Projects and optional site photos stay on this iPhone.
